# Feel

Nothing changes state in 0 ms, and nothing slows the person down.

Give each class of object its own motion rule. Small controls respond fast, panels settle, and overshoot appears only where it means something.

**Why.** Motion connects an action to its result and keeps the person oriented. Waiting for it breaks that connection.

Apply this principle only to work with a surface a person sees or touches, including a visual or terminal interface. Skip changes with no such surface without a note.
